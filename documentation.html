How to Use: Recommended Courses

Your personalized advising schedule and section capacity tracker

1. Real-Time Seat Status

Instantly see how many seats are taken vs available. Color-coded badges highlight whether sections are open, limited (5 or fewer left), or full.

2. Multi-Field Filter & Search

Type any course code, faculty initials, classroom number, or time slot to instantly narrow down your recommended routine.

3. 1-Click PDF Schedule Export

Click Export PDF to download a landscape printable timetable containing course codes, faculty names, rooms, and class times.

4. Available Only Mode

Toggle the Available Only switch to hide full sections and focus exclusively on open slots during advising.

How to Use: Department-Wise Course Fetch

Search and filter across all university departments and semesters

1. Multi-Department Selection

Select one, several, or all departments simultaneously using the checkboxes or quick search bar.

2. Choose Academic Semester

Select your desired semester from the dropdown to load official course listings and time schedules.

3. Dynamic Department Toggle

Inside the course explorer, open Manage Departments to toggle departments on or off without reloading the entire page.

4. Sorting & Pagination

Sort by course code, most seats remaining, or class time slot. Choose page sizes from 25 to All entries.

Auto-Retry During Advising Rush

No manual refreshes needed when the university portal is busy

When thousands of students log in at the same time, university servers often return busy or temporary timeout responses. This website includes an automated retry system:

Zero Page Refreshes Keeps your filters and inputs active while attempting next connections in the background.
Live Retry Counter Shows clear status feedback with retry attempt count and elapsed seconds.
Instant Fail-Safe If student ID or password is incorrect, retry stops immediately to notify you.
